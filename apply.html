What We Offer & What We Ask

Before you complete your application, take a moment to understand everything that comes with joining DriveElite — the privileges we extend to every driver in our network, and the professional standards we uphold together across the European Union.

What We Bring to the Table
Your Vehicle, Fully Provided
We supply the right vehicle for your licence category — whether that's a sleek passenger car, a high-capacity delivery scooter, a fully equipped freight truck, or a modern touring coach. You drive. We cover the wheels.
Designed for Your Lifestyle
Choose shifts that suit your rhythm. Morning routes, evening runs, part-time or full-time — your schedule is yours to shape. We work around your life, not the other way around.
Weekly Pay + Performance Rewards
Reliable income deposited every Friday. Base salary plus performance-based commissions and loyalty bonuses that grow the longer you stay with us. The harder you drive, the more you earn — simple, fair, transparent.
Full Support & Coverage
24/7 driver support line, comprehensive insurance on every vehicle, roadside assistance across all EU territories, and a dedicated account manager to handle the admin so you can focus on the road ahead.
Our Professional Standards
Minimum Age — 18 Years
All applicants must be at least 18 years of age at the time of registration, in full compliance with European driving regulations across all member states.
Valid European Driving Licence
You must hold a current, non-suspended driving licence issued by an EU member state or a country recognised under international equivalency agreements. Expired or restricted licences are not accepted.
Two Years of Experience — Preferred for C & D
While Category A and B positions are open to newly licensed drivers, HGV freight and passenger transport roles require a minimum of two years of documented professional driving experience, ensuring the safety of all road users.
Commitment to Conduct & Legal Compliance
Every DriveElite driver is expected to adhere to European labour regulations, national road traffic laws, and our Code of Professional Conduct — upholding the highest standards of safety, punctuality, and client respect at all times.
